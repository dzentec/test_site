// The Depth Behind The Work

Software is easy to prototype.
Products are not.

A software decision can change the BOM. A hardware decision can change the cloud architecture. Production constraints can change the product itself. Cross-disciplinary engineering experience allows us to produce specifications that survive contact with real implementation.
